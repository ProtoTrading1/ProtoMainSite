# 2026-10-01

## Product popup: variant arrows and "Variant X of N" label

**Time:** 12:00 SAST

**Summary.** The product popup for variant groups now has left/right arrows on
the image and a label at the top of the image ("Variant 2 of 16" plus the
variant's short name, e.g. "Bronze"). The arrows step through the group's
variants and wrap at either end. Each step selects the variant, so the image,
title, SKU, price and stock all switch, and the matching row scrolls into view
in the variant list. On mobile the image also responds to a left/right swipe;
on desktop the keyboard ←/→ keys do the same (ignored while typing in the
quantity box). The short name is the variant's colour when set, otherwise the
last `|` segment of its name.

This applies to every product with `isVariantGroup` and more than one variant,
so variant groups loaded later get it automatically with no data change.

**Files:** `src/components/ProductCard.jsx`, `src/index.css`.

**Verification:** `npm run build` passes; `npm test` 425 passed, 0 failed;
eslint on `ProductCard.jsx` adds no new findings (one existing warning). Rendered
the popup in a throwaway harness with Playwright at 1280×800 and 375×740: arrows
and label display without overlapping the tags or close button, arrow clicks and
←/→ keys change variant, including wrap-around.

**Deployment:** pushed to branch `claude/funny-pasteur-2zlarz`; not merged or
deployed.
