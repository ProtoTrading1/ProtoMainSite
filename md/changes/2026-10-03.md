**Changes.** Customer-owned profile updates cannot change authority, identity
or staff fields; browser roles cannot insert their own customer row. New trade
applications require one-time mailbox proof before legacy-customer approval.
Existing approved accounts and staff service-client workflows are preserved.

Basket lines use product plus normalized preference throughout edit, remove,
reorder, sync and review. Duplicate legacy lines retain their combined quantity.
Client and authoritative checkout checks enforce total stock across preferences.

Checkout stores the original request hash and notification snapshot and claims
each delivery channel durably. Conflicting replay is rejected; a matching retry
uses the original captured order and successful channels are not resent. Browser
pending intent is saved before POST and survives reload. Recovery requires an
explicit retry, keeps the original reference and preserves a changed basket
with a visible explanation. Denied recovery storage blocks POST. This local
intent does not synchronize between devices.

Catalogue failures show Retry rather than a successful empty result, and failed
searches are excluded from search-result metrics. Featured, registration,
authentication, reset and checkout requests have deadlines. Reset-link transport
failure is retryable and does not declare the link expired. Public/account render
failures have a global recovery screen; optional map failure is contained.
Existing branding, navigation, assets, search ranking and the admin application
are preserved. Impeccable hardening/clarification guided recovery controls.
